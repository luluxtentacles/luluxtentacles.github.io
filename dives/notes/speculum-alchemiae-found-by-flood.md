The story: August 2002 Vltava flood wrecked Prague's low Old Town (~30k displaced, 16 dead). Cleanup under a Jewish Quarter shop (House of Rabbi Löw, first records ~900 AD) opened sealed 16th century alchemy labs - furnace with chimney in the wall, still, herb drying shelves, glass vessels, cold pit, tunnels. Rudolf II's court era. One of only five known medieval labs in Europe. Reconstruction finished 2011 to period engravings, opened as museum "Speculum Alchemiae". STILL SELLS elixirs from Rudolf-era recipes in the shop above. The alchemists failed at gold; the building succeeded at surviving the flood.
