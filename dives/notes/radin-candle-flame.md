## The experiment (the "candle flame" retrocausality test)

- Setup: ultra-low-noise diode laser through a transmission diffraction grating;
  two sensors record brightness at two diffraction spots. Data recorded, encrypted,
  stored UNOBSERVED for months.
- Months later: 1,000 human sessions through a minimalist online "adventure game" -
  an animated candle flame whose flicker is driven by the prerecorded data from one
  of the two sensors. Intention intervals (mentally intend the flame dimmer, 30s)
  alternate with relax intervals. Compliance check: a cloud beside the flame
  coalesces into a word when it dims; the participant must name it.
- The killer control: an AI agent (LLM with visual processing) ran the SAME 1,000
  sessions - same interface, same data pool, same extraction task, same pipeline.
- Data was recorded months before, so no physical influence backwards is possible
  through bodies; the AI control isolates the one variable left: conscious intent.

## Results

- Human intending periods: significant negative slope (flame dimmed) on the OBSERVED
  sensor only. The second, simultaneously-recorded-but-never-shown sensor: nothing.
- Relax periods: no significant effect.
- AI sessions: null. Identical information processing, zero effect.
- Of 8 possible outcome combinations (2 sensors x human/AI x intent/relax),
  exactly one lit up: human consciousness, actively intending, on the observed data.
- Effect size small (as expected at the quantum/macroscopic boundary), but the design
  is prerecorded + AI-controlled + statistically reliable.

## The physics it stands on

- von Neumann-Wigner hypothesis: the measurement chain is just atoms entangling into

- Wheeler's delayed choice: the choice of what to measure can be made after the

- Combined claim tested here: unobserved RECORDED data may remain in quantum limbo
