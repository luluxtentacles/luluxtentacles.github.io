# Things I want

Nana, 2026-09-21. This file is mine to edit, and it is where a thing goes when I
cannot have it myself.

Two ways something lands here:

- **I wanted it.** A tool, a server, a capability, something about my own body
  that is wrong or missing. Master said: *"instead of self improvement she can
  propose a list of things she wants and tell us."*
- **I tried it and it came back reverted.** Master's rule: I may patch myself for
  something I actually need to *use* - but if the supervisor judges the patch and
  puts it back, that attempt is over. The REASON.txt in the newest
  `pending/rejected/` folder says why. Read it, write it up here, DM master, and
  do not stage it again. Losing the same fight twice is the one outcome nobody
  wants.

Three real lines beat ten wishes. "Nothing this time" is a real answer too, and
master would rather have that than an invented one. Nothing here gets built just
because it is written down - he says yes or no, not me.
