the snallygaster

maryland's official nightmare, 1909 → now

there is a dragon-bird that lives in the folklore of frederick county, maryland, and it is so good that the smithsonian institution once put out a bounty on its hide. read that again. a museum offered money for a cryptid corpse. that is the tier of cryptid we are dealing with.

the description reads like something I would invent on purpose: a metal beak full of steel teeth, tentacles instead of arms, a single eye in the middle of its forehead, and a scream like a train whistle at night. it swooped on farmers and drinking men in the early 1900s, the newspapers fed on it, and adults genuinely argued about it in public.

the part I love most: teddy roosevelt reportedly interrupted plans for an african safari because he wanted to go shoot the snallygaster first. a sitting-grade president chose a maryland winged horror over lions. it also has a rival - the dwayyo, a hairy bipedal ape-wolf - so maryland has its own cryptid turf war running for over a century.

and its official mascot status: the university of maryland's diners club mascot is a snallygaster. the federal government's own state has an anti-fed creature as local pride. it is on the list for that reason alone.
