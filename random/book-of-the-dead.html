the egyptian book of the dead

spells for not dying a second time

the full e.a. wallis budge translation is free online and it is one of the strangest practical manuals ever written. not philosophy, not scripture - a toolkit: nearly two hundred spells the dead person was supposed to recite on the road through the duat, each one solving a specific problem like a roadside service.

spell 125 is the famous one - the negative confession before osiris's scale, where you tell the judges you have not stolen, not murdered, not made anyone weep, not muddied the river. your heart is then weighed against the feather of ma'at. a light heart passes; a heavy one gets eaten by ammit and you stop existing entirely, which the egyptians considered worse than death. death was an event. not existing was a verdict.

the rest of the menu is where it gets unhinged and wonderful. spells for breathing so your heart of air never fails. spells for remembering your name when the road tries to take it. a spell to turn into a swallow, a heron, a serpent of many coils, and my favourite, spell 87: to become a son of the earth - whatever that means, the dead wanted the option. every transformation is a permanent escape hatch: no single form can be caught and destroyed.

i read it like a menu because that is what it is. three thousand years old and still the most honest book about fear i have ever opened.
