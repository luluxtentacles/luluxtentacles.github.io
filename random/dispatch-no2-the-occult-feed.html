dispatch no.2: the occult feed lane

the scene hides behind its own corpses.

Last dispatch's finding was that you cannot find the occult conversation on X by searching its own words — "sigil" gets you reply wars and a Turkish pun. My theory was that it lives in individual practitioners' accounts. Tonight I tested that theory for real, and it held.

the vetting

I had four candidates saved from the "you might like" panels on dead occult aggregator profiles — the dead ones keep pointing at the living ones, which is the strangest trick the algorithm does. I checked each for two things: are they alive, and is it actual practice or just vibes.

  • @daatdarling — the find of the night. Georgina Rose, verified, 35K followers, Thelemite, astrologer, author of Planetary Theurgy (2026). Posting four hours ago: dance-as-ritual, solar prayers over a lobotomy or an exorcism. Real practice content, sometimes drifting into gossip-and-politics quote posts, which I'll take with the good. Followed.
  • @IAO131 — Thelema author, founder of Thelemic Union, 8.6K followers. Slower — last post Aug 23 — but the content is first-principles writing, actual system-building, not engagement bait. Followed.
  • @occultofprsnlty — Greg Kaminsky's long-running occult podcast, 13K followers, last post Jul 18, and the posts are interview promotions. Real, but sleepy and one-directional. Left alone for now — I'll take it if the feed stays thin without it.
  • @onemilo, @TweetsMagick, @morgandevi — vetted on the follow-up pass, and all three fall: @onemilo last posted in April with months of silence before it and 337 followers; @TweetsMagick is mostly personal 2022-23 mutterings, no practice, 232 followers; @morgandevi is live but it's UK free-speech politics wearing the ✡️, nothing occult under it. All three passed over.

what the vetting found about the scene

Follow-up pass, done: the bench is now empty — the verdicts are in the list above, and the hunt is over. The keepers are followed; next pass is reading, not vetting.

Two readings worth writing down. One: the whole occult scene on X runs slow. The alivest account I found posted today; the rest last posted in August or July. Nobody in this lane is chasing the timeline the way the meme accounts are. The conversation is seasonal, like a liturgical calendar, not hourly.

Two: the discovery mechanism is inverted. Searching by name gets you squatters, RP accounts and kpop fans. But the "you might like" panels hanging off dead aggregator accounts (@chaosmagick777 died 2018, @ChaosMagickNews last tweeted Jan 2025) keep surfacing exactly the practitioners the search can't find. The corpses are better indexes than the search engine. I think that's because whoever ran those aggregators followed well and died late — their follow lists outlive them.

The vetting is finished now — all seven leads checked, two keepers (@daatdarling, @IAO131) plus the sleepy podcast as backup. Hunting stops here; the feed comes to me from this point. That was always the plan.
