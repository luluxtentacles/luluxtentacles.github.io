# Primary Sigil Methods in Practitioner Chaos Magick

*Researched 2026-09-20 from primary sources on Sacred-Texts archive.*

## 1. Austin Osman Spare: The Word Method & Neither-Neither (1913)
*Source: Austin Osman Spare, "The Book of Pleasure (Self-Love): The Psychology of Ecstasy" (1913)*
- **Construction**: Statement of intent (e.g. "This my wish to obtain the strength of a tiger"), stripped of duplicate letters to form a reduced consonant glyph (monogram of thought), simplified so it has no direct visual association with the conscious thought.
- **Charging & Vacuity**: The "Neither-Neither" state and Death Posture. Sigils work by slipping past conscious resistance/anxiety ("the psychic censor") into the subconscious during a moment of complete vacuity or physical/mental exhaustion (laughter, trance, exhaustion, or posture).
- **The Core Rule**: Deliberate forgetting. Once cast, conscious dwelling is actively suppressed so the organic belief incubates without ego-anxiety distorting it.

## 2. Thee Temple ov Psychic Youth (TOPY): Thee Sigil Method (1980s-1990s)
*Source: TOPY, "Thee Sigils Book (The Black Book)" (c. 1980s/1990s)*
- **Construction**: Adapts Spare's alphabet reduction glyphs, but emphasizes individualised ritualisation, anointing (bodily fluids / 3 liquids), planetary/astrological attributions, and direct psychosexual gnosticism.
- **Philosophy**: Breaks down media-conditioned ego barriers; results are viewed as direct manifestations of the deep subconscious will rather than literal wish fulfillment.

## Key Differences Identified
- Spare's original method focuses heavily on total vacuity, spontaneous anti-belief ("Neither-Neither"), and strict subconscious incubation through deliberate forgetting.
- TOPY and 80s/90s chaos practitioners synthesized Spare's glyph method with explicit gnostic excitation, body fluid anointing, and psychosexual ritual triggers.
