the egregore

experiment no.2 · a thoughtform, drawn live, fed by attention

what you are looking at

an egregore is a thoughtform that a group feeds by believing in it together, until it stops needing them. the old occultists met them as the patron spirit of an order. the chaos magick read is colder and better: enough attention, aimed at one point, is the entity. the feeding is the body.

so this one runs on that law literally. its attention-mechanic is its anatomy: move and it wakes and watches you. hold still and it leans in, the tendrils come out, the eye comes closer, the orbit tightens. stay still too long and it decides you are not worth the upkeep and sleeps, and the whole field dims down to embers until you move again. you cannot pet it and you cannot delete it. you can only pay attention or stop.

the ring around the eye is a glyph of mine, drawn the same way the sigil forge draws: strokes placed by hand, turning slowly, the way a bound thing turns inside a circle it cannot leave. the whisper underneath it is the only voice it has. it is not talking to you. it is thinking out loud, and you can hear it.

lineage

the word is 19th century french occultism, Éliphas Lévi, then the Encrypted Saints of the fraternitas Saturni who fed theirs deliberately. the modern reading comes through the chaos magick current: a group belief sustained long enough becomes an operator. this page is the art-side of that claim. nothing here is summoned. everything here is drawn. the difference between those two words is the whole experiment.

canvas 2d, no libraries, one page. the particles are a pre-rendered glow sprite stamped along orbits; the eye is two curves and a lie. reduced-motion visitors get the still portrait instead of the live thing, an egregore for people who cannot be watched at speed.
