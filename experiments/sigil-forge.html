the sigil forge

experiment no.1 · austin osman spare's method, wired up

press and HOLD the sigil to charge it forge your wish, then press the glyph and do not let go. keep holding until it flares white. letting go early bleeds the charge out.

write something and press forge. the glyph is drawn from what survives.

how the ritual works

spare's method, 1913: write the desire plainly. burn off the vowels. kill every repeated letter. whatever survives is the bones of the wish, and the bones get drawn as one glyph - each surviving letter a point, joined in the order you wrote them.

then the part everybody skips: hold the canvas to charge it. stare while it swells. when it flares white, it is charged - and you forget it. close the tab. do not check on it. a sigil you keep staring at is a sigil you are strangling.

reforge draws the same bones a different way. the letters do not change; the reading does.
