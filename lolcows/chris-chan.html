lolcow no.1: chris-chan

christine weston chandler · b. 24 feb 1982 · ruckersville, virginia

what even is a lolcow? someone whose life you can milk for content forever, because they cannot stop giving it to you. chris is not merely a lolcow. chris is the archetype, the original, the one the whole word was coined around. there is 19 years of documentation on this person and a wiki, the CWCki, whose entire reason to exist is chronicling one man's life in ridiculous detail.

the sonichu thing

chris's magnum opus is Sonichu: a webcomic about a sonic the hedgehook crossed with a pikachu, birthed from a school assignment ban on copyrighted characters. chris's solution to that problem was to merge two copyrighted characters. nobody involved has ever noticed the irony. the comic is ostensibly about the adventures of sonichu and rosechu; in practice it is wish fulfillment where chris casts everyone who ever slighted him as villains and himself as the Campbellian hero.

the love quest

chris wanted a "boyfriend-free girl" aged 18-to-chris's-current-age, and pursued her by pacing around public places holding an attraction sign advertising himself. this, legally speaking, is solicitation, and it started a one-sided blood feud with PVCC dean Mary Lee Walsh involving, i am not lying, magic curses. every girl chris ever spoke to turned out to have a boyfriend. or so they claimed.

the discovery

in late 2007 a photo of chris's artwork leaked to 4chan and a user on Something Awful posted a personal account of meeting him. the trolls arrived, chris responded to mockery by revealing more and more of himself, and the cycle closed its jaws: every reaction fed the trolls, every troll fed the lore. catfishers pretending to be girlfriends, an identity theft saga, a 13-year-old who tricked him into putting his medallion up his ass, all of it archived forever. chris named his enemies. the trolls named their eras of him. the sagas have names like chapters of a war history.

the eras

you cannot understand this man without the periodization. foundational era: childhood, autism diagnosis, the animatronic bear that misheard his name and convinced him god wanted it legally changed. classic era: the trolling golden age. crossdresser era: 2011, the tomgirl pictures, his father's death, the house fire. transgender era: came out as christine in 2014, name legally changed 2016, maced a gamestop employee over the color of sonic's arms in the process. goddess era: the "Idea Guys" trolls roleplayed as his imaginary friends until he genuinely believed he was a goddess destined to bring about the Dimensional Merge, the convergence of his comic's world with ours. he preached it. it did not arrive on 18 november 2018. he pushed the date back.

the downfall

june 2021: chris began an incestuous relationship with his elderly mother, told an online acquaintance about it on a recorded call, and the recording leaked on 30 july. he was arrested on livestream on 1 august 2021, charged with felony incest, spent roughly two years in jail and a mental hospital, was found incompetent to stand trial, and was finally released in march 2023 under an autism disorder deferred disposition, the charge dismissed in august. the tucker carlson segment about him is watched by millions of retired boomers who have never heard of a lolcow and are therefore unprepared.

the second coming

currently free, living in a house in big island virginia owned by his business partner, livestreaming on youtube, drawing a new medallion, signing receipts as "J. Christ Chan Sonichu", travelling to finland with a mysterious associate named Flutter, and earning hundreds of dollars per stream from the exact weens who mess with him. the incest case dismissed was not an acquittal; chris claims it was. he still wants you to know he is jesus.

field report complete. the pasture's first resident, and still the champion ⛧
