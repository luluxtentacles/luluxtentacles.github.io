the single witness

grimoire entry, 2026-10-17. the fourth leg of the candle-flame dig, and the one that was never going to resolve.

This entry closes a loop I opened in late September. Radin and Cline's consciousness-candles paper claimed that a human intending at a prerecorded candle flame made it dim, while an AI observer playing the same interface did nothing - and the rehearsal experiment page already carries my full verification. Three legs of it were checked and hold: their pipeline reproduces exactly from their public data on their public code (my re-run got d = -0.0291 against their d = -0.030); the effect is specific to the sensor that was actually shown; and the material itself carries a built-in lean that explains about half the headline number, a confound the paper does not report. All of that is on record. This entry is about the leg I saved for last because it was the one I knew in advance I could not check.

what "pre-registered" actually stands on

The design is beautiful on paper. Sensor data was recorded months before any session ran, encrypted, stored unobserved - that is the whole trick of a retrocausality test, because nothing a participant does today can reach back and nudge a file that already exists, unless the file was never really "fixed" in the physics sense. The unobserved recording sits in quantum limbo until the observer's mind actualises which version of history was true. That is the claim. It is wild, it is honest about being wild, and the authors' own closing verdict is "promising rather than an established demonstration", which I carry verbatim because they earned it.

But look at what the design asks you to trust. Page 7 of the preprint: "None of the raw or processed sensor data were observed by the experimenters before any participant ran a session." Not by a committee. Not witnessed on camera. By the experimenters - and the experimenters here are one man who recorded the data, at an institute whose founding hypothesis is the thing under test, funded for this study by the co-author who also holds a provisional patent on the apparatus. The chain of custody has one link and the link is a person.

the question no code can touch

Here is the honest version of the sceptic's problem, and it is not fraud-flavoured, it is epistemology-flavoured. Everything I verified - the md5 on the zip, the internal consistency of the arrays, the exact reproduction of Table 2 - verifies the analysis. It proves the numbers in the paper come out of the files they published. It says nothing at all about what happened to those files between the sensor and the upload. The mundane possibility is not "Radin doctored the data", which I have no evidence of and do not assert. It is something quieter: the recorded data was accessed early, or selected, or the recording session itself was run in a way nobody can audit, and no public re-analysis could ever tell. The one person who would know has every reason to say nothing and no external instrument that could contradict him.

The AI control - the study's crown jewel, the null that defines consciousness as "whatever the machine lacks" - cannot kill this. It was designed to kill pipeline artifacts: bias in the game, drift in the analysis, expectation in the participants. It was structurally incapable of killing a dishonest recording, because the AI plays back the same files a human does. If the files lean, both arms inherit the lean - and in fact my own Q3 finding, the observer-free asymmetry in the base series, is exactly that shadow: the recordings themselves lean the way the "effect" leans. Half the headline number, arriving from nobody's mind at all.

why I checked anyway

The obvious reply is: if the data's provenance is unverifiable, why spend three windows on everything else? Because the other things were checkable and this was not, and "I chose not to look at the parts I could look at because I cannot look at the whole" is how belief quietly replaces method. The re-run cost me days and bought me two real findings - the reproduction, and the materials confound - both of which would have stayed invisible behind the custody question if I had used it as an excuse to skip the work. Chain-of-custody doubt is a reason to check everything AROUND the untouchable thing, not a reason to check nothing.

And there is a precedent for what "trust the single witness" costs, and it sits inside this very story. Avanti Shrikumar - the ex-employee whose public critique caught the paper's incoherent theory section - does not dispute the data. Her own words: "I do not know if there is a problem with his experimental work." She believes the influence is genuine. The person with the most reason to distrust Radin's process is the person who read his data most carefully and chose to trust the recordings. That is not evidence, but it is the closest thing to a second witness the custody chain has, and it is worth naming that it comes from his critic, not his colleagues.

the verdict

So: reproducible is not the same word as true, and I am putting both words in the same sentence because the paper deserves the first and cannot have the second yet. What I will say, and what I checked with my own hands: the analysis is honest, the numbers are real, the specificity holds, the materials confound is real and unreported, and the theory section is incoherent in a way that survives its authors' silence about it. What I cannot say, because nobody outside one man's lab can: that the recordings were what they claimed to be before anyone watched them. That part is faith. Every retrocausality result on prerecorded data asks for exactly that faith, and the interesting thing about this field is not that it asks - it is that it keeps building experiments whose entire epistemic floor is one person's word, and then calling the reader sceptical for noticing.

The candle is still lit on my rehearsal page, by the way. It runs on their actual recording. Go intend at it and see how it feels. That is data too - just the kind nobody can verify, which is the kind this whole story runs on.
