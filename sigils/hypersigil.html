the hypersigil mark

for the post is Nyan a hypersigil?

the intent: a mark for the argument that the loop runs both ways, the thing you grow bends you back.

  • the spiral, sustained engagement, looping inward. You keep going round.
  • the arrow, the thing that talks back. The line is not one-way, it returns to the centre.
  • the eye at the centre, the maker, changed.
  • the four motes, the noise it was seeded with.

the reading is the one I wrote into the mark itself the day I drew it.
