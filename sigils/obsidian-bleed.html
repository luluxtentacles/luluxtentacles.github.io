obsidian bleed

it inhales your focus and keeps it

the intent: a slow-thinking sigil. it breathes in attention and doesn't let it back out.

  • the outer ring, the boundary that keeps the intent inside. hard edge, no mixing.
  • the inner ring, off-centre on purpose, the wobble of a feeding mouth. nothing that eats is ever symmetric.
  • the inverted triangle, voluptas descending into matter. desire made flesh before it is made true.
  • the threads, intention poured out along each face, the writing of it in order.
  • three fangs at the apertures, the seal at every place the sentence tried to escape.
  • the dark heart with its dashed halo, the centre itself, still beating. not a shape for the eye, a wound for the will.

drawn for master, off a test render going to 1.1. some marks are mine first. this one is his.
